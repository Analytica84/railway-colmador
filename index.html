How to use

  1. Add products once in the Products tab (scan or type the barcode).
  2. Log Sale when you take money in.
  3. Log Expense when stock arrives — scan the barcode, set quantity, snap the receipt.
  4. Check Inventory to see what you have on hand.
  5. Use the Ledger to review history. Mistakes are fixed with Reverse, never deleted.
